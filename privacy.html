PRIVATE FRIENDS GROUP · VERSION 24 OCTOBER 2026

Privacy & data.

A free personal project for invited friends.

Who runs this?

The friend who invited you organises this group. Contact them through your existing group chat about access, corrections, deletion or concerns. This website is intended for a closed, noncommercial group of actual friends.

What the group sees.

A nickname and character; an optional quit clock and nicotine reports; posts, comments and reactions; shifts; game scores, highest waves, longest raid times, extraction/death counts, cosmetics, arcade votes, short-game fragments and shared boss contributions. Everyone admitted with the group code can select, edit, export or delete characters. This is a trust-based group, not individual protected accounts.

Optional nicotine information can reveal health-related information. Use nicknames, avoid identifying detail, and only share your own information or information another person agreed you may share. Games-only characters do not claim nicotine-free time.

Your choice.

Choose optional nicotine sharing in Privacy & data inside the app. You can instead use games and chat. Changing that choice stops future nicotine check-ins on that device. Disable a character’s quit clock in its employee card to stop its passive tracking; existing records remain until the character is deleted.

Delete a character from its squad card to erase its profile and associated nicotine reports, posts and replies, shifts, game receipts and cosmetics. Aggregate campaign totals and frozen roster sizes remain without that character’s individual records. Exports are available from character cards. Deletion cannot recall copies another friend already made, and provider logs/backups can follow the provider’s retention rules.

Where records go.

Supabase stores the group database and runs anonymous device authentication. GitHub Pages serves the website. The Supabase JavaScript SDK is served with the website; it is not loaded from a separate CDN. If the organiser enables bot protection, Cloudflare Turnstile also processes the bot check. Providers can process network/security information such as IP addresses under their own policies. This app adds no advertising or analytics.

Device storage.

The app uses operational browser storage for the anonymous session and security/access state, a character choice only when you ask it to remember, saved coping timers you start, settings you select, and sample data when you deliberately enter the demo. A saved nicotine choice is tied to the anonymous device session in Supabase. There are no advertising IDs or analytics cookies added by the app.

Remembering a character is optional and off until selected. Forget the remembered character in Privacy & data. Clock out to revoke this device’s group admission and clear the local Auth session. Clear this site’s browser data to remove other saved local settings and demo data. The anonymous identity cannot be recovered on another device without re-entering the shared group code.

Retention and limitations.

Character records are kept to support that character’s quit journey and game history until the character is deleted. Removing a chat message deletes it and associated replies and drawing bytes from the active database. The newest 10,000 messages and replies are retained; old individual messages are removed when that bound is exceeded. Drawings have a separate short retention policy below.

The guide describes common nicotine withdrawal patterns; it does not diagnose, measure nicotine levels, or predict recovery. The game’s breath meter, ranks, bosses and attributes are fictional. The coping timer cannot guarantee an alarm while a phone browser is suspended. Speak with a healthcare professional if symptoms worry you.

Free cosmetics, no gambling economy.

There are no purchases, paid entries, bets, tradeable items, cash-outs, monetary prizes or real-world rewards. Random capsules are free, nontransferable cosmetic counters with no resale value or gameplay advantage. Base rarity odds are visible in the stash, and greed’s filtering effect is disclosed.

Crew Chat and drawing challenges

Messages and drawings are shared only with admitted friends. Chat keeps at most 10,000 text messages and replies. Up to 100 drawings are retained for seven days within the shared gallery byte budget; expired drawings are removed on subsequent chat activity. The text prompt can remain. Full-quality PNG originals are uploaded in resumable pieces. Small previews can appear automatically in Overview; originals download only on request. The studio has no stroke, point or time cap. Old gallery files expire to keep stored artwork within a 96 MiB budget. Artwork is not sent to an image-recognition or AI service. Unfinished drawings stay on the device. Choosing an outcome posts the completed drawing, but the outcome is never included in the chat message. An explicit resistance check-in is saved only when nicotine sharing and tracking are enabled. Choosing Not this time never creates a nicotine-use report.

Posting devices can remove their own character’s messages. Character deletion cascades to its posts and drawings. An export includes retained drawings. The permanent shared noticeboard title and text autosave for admitted friends and stay until the crew changes them. Conflicting edits preserve the local draft instead of silently overwriting another person. History and chat downloads are bounded to reduce free-plan usage; they cannot guarantee that the whole project will never reach a usage limit.

BLACKSITE expedition progress

Confirmed district clears, weapon blueprints and personal bests follow your selected character. Run tickets remain device-bound and use checkpoints; temporary combat equipment resets each expedition. Character export includes expedition progress and character deletion removes it. Practice and the isolated demo do not create live records or nicotine reports.

Optional team affiliation & aura atelier (v3.5)

Weekly Nightfall teams are voluntary affiliations visible to admitted friends, not verified health status. Joining never enables nicotine tracking. Confirmed games, artwork, optional explicit resistance reports, Rally and other-device post appreciation can contribute bounded team damage. Nicotine use, dose and strength earn no competitive reward. Membership locks for the weekly Helsinki operation; leaving hides the affiliation while its target and earned damage remain for that operation. Team receipts, roster records, aura stardust and mutation receipts follow the character, are included in export and are removed by character deletion. Device identifiers used for limits are not included in standings.

Original ten-tier capsule inventories and equipped choices are preserved. Only duplicate auras can be distilled; crafting spends earned stardust on a specified missing tier. Auras change appearance only. Music, effects, volumes and atmospheric-motion preferences stay on the device; sound begins only after a deliberate interaction. Synthesized tracks use no third-party audio service. Reduced motion overrides atmospheric animation.
