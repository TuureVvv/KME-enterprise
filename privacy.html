PRIVATE FRIENDS GROUP · VERSION 24 OCTOBER 2026

Privacy & data.

A free personal project for invited friends.

Who runs this?

The friend who invited you organises this group. Contact them through your existing group chat about access, corrections, deletion or concerns. This website is intended for a closed, noncommercial group of actual friends.

What the group sees.

A nickname and character; an optional quit clock and nicotine reports; posts, comments and reactions; shifts; game scores, cosmetics, arcade votes, short-game fragments and shared boss contributions. Everyone admitted with the group code can select, edit, export or delete characters. This is a trust-based group, not individual protected accounts.

Optional nicotine information can reveal health-related information. Use nicknames, avoid identifying detail, and only share your own information or information another person agreed you may share. Games-only characters do not claim nicotine-free time.

Your choice.

Choose optional nicotine sharing in Privacy & data inside the app. You can instead use games and chat. Changing that choice stops future nicotine check-ins on that device. Disable a character’s quit clock in its employee card to stop its passive tracking; existing records remain until the character is deleted.

Delete a character from its squad card to erase its profile and associated nicotine reports, posts and replies, shifts, game receipts and cosmetics. Aggregate campaign totals and frozen roster sizes remain without that character’s individual records. Exports are available from character cards. Deletion cannot recall copies another friend already made, and provider logs/backups can follow the provider’s retention rules.

Where records go.

Supabase stores the group database and runs anonymous device authentication. GitHub Pages serves the website. The Supabase JavaScript SDK is served with the website; it is not loaded from a separate CDN. If the organiser enables bot protection, Cloudflare Turnstile also processes the bot check. Providers can process network/security information such as IP addresses under their own policies. This app adds no advertising or analytics.

Device storage.

The app uses operational browser storage for the anonymous session and security/access state, a character choice only when you ask it to remember, saved coping timers you start, settings you select, and sample data when you deliberately enter the demo. A saved nicotine choice is tied to the anonymous device session in Supabase. There are no advertising IDs or analytics cookies added by the app.

Remembering a character is optional and off until selected. Forget the remembered character in Privacy & data. Clock out to revoke this device’s group admission and clear the local Auth session. Clear this site’s browser data to remove other saved local settings and demo data. The anonymous identity cannot be recovered on another device without re-entering the shared group code.

Retention and limitations.

Character records are kept to support that character’s quit journey and game history until the character is deleted. Removing a single chat message hides it from the room but can retain a private deleted record until character deletion. The visible feed is limited to recent conversations; that is not an automatic erasure schedule.

The guide describes common nicotine withdrawal patterns; it does not diagnose, measure nicotine levels, or predict recovery. The game’s breath meter, ranks, bosses and attributes are fictional. The coping timer cannot guarantee an alarm while a phone browser is suspended. Speak with a healthcare professional if symptoms worry you.

Free cosmetics, no gambling economy.

There are no purchases, paid entries, bets, tradeable items, cash-outs, monetary prizes or real-world rewards. Random capsules are free, nontransferable cosmetic counters with no resale value or gameplay advantage. Base rarity odds are visible in the stash, and greed’s filtering effect is disclosed.
