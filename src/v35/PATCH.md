# v3.5 — The Dark Side Of NICOTINE!

## NIGHTFALL: weekly team battles

Choose Quitters / Rehab or Night Shift / Nicotine Users, or spectate. Fight the Nicotine Singularity through played games, original artwork, Rally, popular new posts and optional explicit resistance check-ins. Nicotine consumption, pouch counts and nicotine strength never award damage. Joining a crew never enables health tracking.

Teams compare completion percentages against a visible roster target: 60 HP per member per remaining Helsinki day. Momentum rewards earlier active days in the current operation. Character and device caps keep repeated taps and profile switching from farming points. Choices lock until the next Monday; leaving hides affiliation while retaining the week’s totals. Existing crew campaign and finales remain available.

## Your capsules become your look

The wardrobe now includes all ten existing aura tiers, each with a distinct avatar effect, profile backdrop and rarity signature. Showcase them across profiles, the picker, chat and the squad. Preview locked rarities, equip owned ones or remove an aura freely.

Distill duplicate copies into stardust while always preserving the last copy. Craft a specific missing rarity at its displayed fixed price. No extra random purchase or combat advantage. Existing capsule odds, inventories and equipped choices are preserved. Unconfirmed actions keep a device-local receipt across refresh, so retrying spends materials only once.

## The after-hours observatory

Navy, ivory, cyan, violet and amber replace the pervasive green tint; KME’s lime accents and familiar navigation remain. The cosmic backdrop, orbital details, readable surfaces, focus rings and responsive interactions support both desktop and phones. The support controls stay before the new battle strip on mobile.

## Original game music & sound controls

BLACKSITE, the rotating arcade and TWEAKMAX have distinct synthesized tracks. Music and sound effects have independent toggles and volumes, remembered on the device. Music follows real play and stops during menus, pauses and hidden tabs. Sound starts only after deliberately enabling it. Atmospheric motion can be disabled; system reduced motion takes priority.

All v3.0 BLACKSITE weapons, modifications, districts, enemies, shop, loot, progression and Drawing Studio improvements remain included.

## Data and release

The backend update is additive and keeps old clients working. New tables use closed RLS, revoked direct access and the existing admitted RPC model. Export and character deletion include new progression. Tests use isolated demo/database fixtures, never fake crew records. Friendly competition still uses the established shared-character model; it is not individual-account anti-cheat.

Actual publication and verification status are recorded in `outputs/DEPLOYMENT-v3.5.json`, not inferred from these release notes.
