Field manual · controls, classes and survival

Move: WASD/arrows or left stick. Aim/fire: mouse + hold click, or right stick. Space: dash. R: parry. Q: class gadget. E: contextual interaction. V: infusion. F: cycle owned weapons. X: extract. Tab: map. Escape: pause. Hold and RELEASE fire for charged beams, rings and rail shots. There is no weapon heat or venting.

You start with 90 seconds of quiet scavenging. Cleared assaults grant 60 seconds; start the next assault early for a shop voucher. Quiet time does not raise enemy difficulty. Restore three signals, choose field upgrades, finish looting, call and defeat the guardian and extract to unlock the next district. You can evacuate early after one minute, but that does not clear the district. Dash away from a locked eruption; parry shots back at their owners; attack Sludgemass from behind; punish Mr. Potency during reload.

Dropped equipment stays for the run and appears as a diamond on the map. Press E or INTERACT to read it, then install, pack or recycle. Buildings offer three rewards; elite vaults require defeating a guard. Loot decisions pause the action. Bongo stocks six rotating offers; parts buy research choices. The shelter is your tactical home. Turrets, repairs, cryo/Tesla equipment and reactive barricades use run scrap. Leaving it unattended is a decision. The alarmed purple cache holds a prototype: carry it home, then extract to keep the Firing Squad blueprint. It slows you slightly and calls an enemy patrol.

After the guardian dies, leave with the clear or accept an overtime contract: +35% eligible score within existing caps, an elite patrol, and a longer extraction channel. Ordinary overtime still gets harder. No normal-run time limit. Death or retreat retains 20% of eligible haul. Only confirmed successful extraction saves clears and blueprints.

These operatives are loadouts inside your existing character, not extra KME profiles. Practice is safe and earns nothing. Pausing keeps the expedition frozen while the tab stays open; there is no suspended combat-world save. Quitting an active expedition settles a retreat. Unconfirmed results can be retried without duplicate rewards.
