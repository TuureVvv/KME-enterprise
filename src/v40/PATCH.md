# v4.0 — NO MORE AURA FOR U

- **A fresh aura collection:** collectible/equipped auras restart once for everyone. The previous collection is privately archived for export. Characters, artwork, other cosmetics, health records, scores and BLACKSITE unlocks remain.
- **One stardust wallet:** old confetti and aura stardust merge 1:1. Existing cosmetic earning/spending and named-aura crafting use the same balance.
- **24 standard auras:** previews, owned-first browsing, rarity filtering and rarity-coloured cards. Every normal capsule uses the same independent rarity pool; Aether is exactly 0.1%, regardless of greed. Variants are uniform within their tier.
- **Duplicates worth keeping:** visual resonance grows at 1/3/6/10/15 copies. Distilling pays only 1–12 stardust by tier, preserves the final copy and asks before reducing resonance. Effects never add combat power.
- **Six hidden signatures:** free, rare, limited appearances around different app surfaces, outside the shop/capsule pool. Eligible inspections have a 1-in-128 chance, once per character and device per Helsinki day. No paid retries.
- **My profile:** character identity, faction, original rank emblems, optional rank display, game records, achievements, best-loved retained drawing, collection/style and Journey/activity tabs. Crew management lives in a small closed directory. Rank names stay unchanged.
- **Drawing and chat:** visible Like drawing buttons reuse existing votes across Chat/Overview/replies. Brush smoothing has an exact zero/off setting, flushes the final point and recovers with the draft. Existing opacity and movable/addable/removable layers remain.
- **Continuous BLACKSITE:** four districts progress automatically after guardian checkpoints, then repeat in harder circuits. The entire build, inventory, perks, resources and run totals carry forward. A 12-second transit window offers extraction; unconfirmed checkpoints stop safely with retry.
- **Distinct missions and threats:** sequential seismic probes, a valve fuse/contested pump/sludge flush, recoverable audit evidence delivered to the Approval Committee's terminal, and shielded executive power cells/breaker. Dredgers, redaction drones and executive escorts join district-specific enemy pools. District lighting/weather stay distinct.
- **Upgrade through the expedition:** Workshop calibration spends run scrap and parts for +22% base weapon damage per level. Requisitions extend to eight levels. Later arrivals supply limited healing/materials; threat increases with sectors/circuits. Stardust never buys weapon power.
- **Competitive records:** no old 10,000-point or elapsed×24 gameplay ceiling. Bigint v4 records track banked score, best sector, kills, runs, extractions and longest run. Death/retreat retain 20%; completed sector checkpoints remain. Legacy records stay labelled separately.
- **BLACKSITE stardust:** confirmed sectors award 5/8/12/20 by district, within a shared 60 daily character/device cap, alongside existing bounded game rewards.
- **Nicotine Singularity:** renamed team event in Overview, Play and profiles, with six weekly themes and contributor-only faction victory achievements. A tie awards Shared Orbit. Operations rotate at Helsinki Monday boundaries.
- **Reporting credit, as clarified:** Night Shift receives two damage per explicit nicotine-use or resistance check-in; Quitters receive two per explicit resistance check-in, including I'm tweaking → I resisted. First five qualifying reports per character/device/day, shared across types. Existing consent is required. Quantity/strength/spending never change the credit; no game or timer silently creates a report.
- **Optional stardust contributions:** 10 stardust → 1 Singularity damage (up to 10/day within the common 60 damage cap), or 20 → up to 3 original crew-boss damage (up to 15/day). Confirm the displayed event before spending. Finale time cannot be purchased.
- **Phone clarity:** compact game header, clear health/stamina, visible aim cursor, no tutorial card over vital status, portrait/landscape controls, reduced-motion support and existing sound controls.

This remains a free private friend-group game. Scores are client-reported with device/receipt validation, not an authoritative anti-cheat simulation. Combat worlds are not saved across refresh; permanent progression and confirmed rewards are.

Guardian reward handoff: the final guardian’s guaranteed modification and salvage automatically pack before transit, with a visible supplies receipt. Unopened rooms and unrelated map drops still reward deliberate exploration.
