# v6.0 — AFTER HOURS: HOSTILE TAKEOVER

Management has left the building. The crew kept the keys.

- **A living headquarters:** a dedicated office world, original illustrated employee badge, six room destinations, a real-record crew gazette, a compact desk launchpad and a keyboard-searchable company directory. The homepage keeps support and conversation within reach.
- **Hostile Takeover:** six departments, cooperative power from verified real game/art/community activity, access-panel puzzles, persistent crew decisions and an authored case file. Six weekly story scenarios rotate on Helsinki Mondays. Claims are capped per character AND device; interrupted actions keep the same receipt. Weekly marks and story artifacts do not alter health, stardust or combat.
- **The Exhibition:** a dedicated searchable/sortable gallery of real retained drawings, artist filtering, on-device favourites, lazy previews and full-size original viewing/download. The seven-day art lifecycle remains explicit.
- **Quiet Room:** an optional two-minute breathing rhythm, five-step grounding sequence, a local coping card and easy routes back to chat, drawing or a pause. No action automatically records a craving or an outcome.
- **Private Logbook:** character/device-scoped notes, recoverable drafts, editing, tags/search, confirmed deletion and JSON export. Notes remain in the browser and can be lost if browser data is cleared; the interface says so.
- **The Night Desk:** an original deterministic daily routing puzzle with ordered stamps, blocked desks, no-crossing paths, keyboard/touch movement, undo, practice and local personal bests. No manufactured shared leaderboard or health credit.
- **Personal desk:** three palettes, comfortable/compact spacing, pinned rooms and reduced effects. System reduced-motion preferences apply.
- **Reliability:** independent bounded text/art response lanes, reset timestamps, deduplicated image requests and a bounded device cache respecting art expiry. Exhausting art downloads no longer shuts down text chat.
- **The complete v5 BLACKSITE update:** briefings, infrastructure, annexes, two original vault bosses, deliberate looting, held transit exploration, build comparisons/identities, practice combinations and adjustable touch controls.

Existing characters, reports, art, scores, unlocks, collections and wallet balances are retained. No new collection reset, nicotine reward change, paid service or automatic crew post. Private notes are local convenience storage, not a secure personal account. BLACKSITE combat does not survive reload; confirmed progression does. Physical phones were not manually tested.
