# v2.2.5 — time to chat out the nicotine

The patch name stays the same. This update puts quitting nicotine back at the centre of Overview.

- **Nicotine support first.** On phones, I'm tweaking and the two nicotine-report actions come immediately after clock-in, ahead of the streak, shared note and conversation. I resisted and I used nicotine stay side by side, above supporting totals. On desktop the support column starts alongside the daily note, instead of below it.
- **One crew feed.** Chat, drawings and recent nicotine check-ins now share Crew updates. Everything, Chat & art and Check-ins filters let you choose what to catch up on. Full chat and full check-in history remain available. The old separate news panel is removed.
- **Write before scrolling.** The message composer is above the feed, with a clear Send action and a direct Full chat link. Messages appear immediately.
- **Phone chat is optional to open.** Crew updates starts collapsed to its heading on phones. Open it to read/post; background refreshes preserve your choice. Closed mobile previews do not fetch artwork. Desktop starts expanded. Switching between phone and desktop layouts resets the sensible default.
- **Better use of space.** Home drawings fill the available chat width instead of leaving an empty strip. Long names/messages wrap, the desktop recent feed has a bounded scroll area, phone content avoids nested scroll boxes, and touch/keyboard targets are clearer. Mobile text inputs use a readable size that avoids focus zoom.
- **Drawing links go where expected.** View chat after posting opens the phone’s collapsed Crew updates panel and selects Chat & art.

The shared daily note keeps its autosave and conflict protection. Full-quality drawings, optional nicotine-sharing consent, BLACKSITE, the rotating game, boss caps and all existing records remain intact. No database migration is required.
