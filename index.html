CHARACTER SHEET · FICTIONAL GAME STATS
What actually counts?

Ranks use continuous nicotine-free time recorded since this character was created, or since the latest quit date or use report, whichever is later. Backdating a quit date cannot create rank time. Your main timer can still show the date you told us.

Only 3 resisted logs per character per UTC day count toward bonus stats. Boss hours are banked once by the server; tap spam and arcade scores cannot create extra hours. Shared profiles and self-reported nicotine use still rely on honesty.

The attributes above are jokes and game stats. They do not measure your health, recovery or willpower.
